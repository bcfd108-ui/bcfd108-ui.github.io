The resort / Hotel Rimrock

A railroad hotel on the rim.

Heavy timber, exposed steel, and basalt give the hotel its character. Inside, a waterfall and a hearth give people two places to agree to meet.

Arriving at the resort

A separate resort drive would bring guests to the hotel’s covered entrance. On foot, guests could enter the park through the resort gate near Firelands.

Upstairs and outdoors

From a balcony on the fifth floor, a guest would look out over the roofs of the works. Downstairs, one courtyard gathers people around the fire. In the other, two people could sit after dinner and hear each other across the table.

Down to the water

Rimrock Company Waterworks would share the hotel building. Hotel guests could walk from their rooms to the water indoors. Waterworks day visitors would have their own entrance.

A railroad hotel has to feed people

The dining plan includes Skip’s for a long dinner, a family diner with booths and a counter, and quick service for morning coffee or a meal between swims. Shared kitchens and laundry support both sides of the building.
