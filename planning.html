The field guide / A family’s day

From the first whistle to the last light.

Imagine a summer day in the park as Gene Jack and the Rimrock team are designing it. A family can spend it together even when only half of them want the next ride.

Morning at the hotel

From the breakfast counter, a child spots the Fire Hall doors going up. Later, in the hub, she takes a Shift Card at the desk. By the time the carousel comes round to her family, she already knows where she wants to go first.

Enter through the hub (opens in a new tab)

On the fire hall porch

An engine draws water on the drill apron. Someone on the porch explains what the crew is watching. Afterward the family boards Fire Call Run together. The coaster riders peel off for Fire Bomber, and the others head for the parachute rigging tables.

Walk into Firelands (opens in a new tab)

Lunch beside the mill pond

A trainload of logs has arrived at Caribel. One mark on one log is easy to remember. From the gallery it is possible to see the carriage take that timber through the saw. Later, over lunch at the Wanigan, somebody asks which stack of boards is theirs.

Follow a log through Caribel (opens in a new tab)

The cool of the mine

In the assay office, a piece of rock can hold a whole family’s attention. Overhead, the headframe is the biggest thing on the street. Then the underground ride turns the view around: the timber is close, the daylight falls away, and now the shaft is above you.

Visit Silver Queen Mine (opens in a new tab)

A dry seat at the water’s edge

At Waterworks one parent stays at a Canteen table while the children head for the Pumphouse. Later they trade places. From the gallery the waiting parent can still see who worked the valve and who was standing underneath.

Spend an afternoon at Waterworks (opens in a new tab)

When the windows light up

After dinner the family has one more choice. Someone wants the courtyard fire. Someone else has been thinking about a warm soak since lunch. The hotel and Springs share a roof, so the walk home is upstairs.
