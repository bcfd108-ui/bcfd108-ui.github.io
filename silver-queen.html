The districts / Silver Queen Mine

What the headframe tells you.

Above ground, the headframe marks the shaft. Nearby, a small assay office explains the question behind the machinery: what is in this rock, and is it worth moving?

Read the skyline

The surface design brings the headframe, ore bins, and hoist into one view. Their relationship gives a visitor a way to understand why a mine needs so much structure above the ground.

At the assay counter

The assay office and mineral gallery make room for a slower visit. A family can follow the path from a sample of rock to a decision about its value, and stay on the district street if they choose.

The underground story

In the proposed ride, daylight gives way to timber sets and the sound of ore cars. After seeing the headframe from the street, a visitor can picture what the machinery overhead is doing for the people below.
