The districts / Firelands

When the bay doors open.

Firelands grows out of the founders’ fire-service experience. Its center is a two-bay fire hall. From the porch, a family could watch the crew check an engine before the attractions begin their day.

The hall and the drill apron

From a bench on the porch, you could watch an engine draw water during a drill. The apron faces the people watching, and the crew has a separate road for a real call. Across the yard, long benches and a tray line make the Mess Hall feel like lunch at fire camp.

Air attack and the jump loft

Fire Bomber is a proposed single-rail coaster with an air-tanker reload base for its station. The Smokejumpers loft shows the work of packing a chute. Fire Call Run gives younger guests a family engine ride, while Fire Tornado is planned near the reservoir.

The lookout and the memorial

The lookout gives visitors a spotter’s view of the district. The memorial has its own quiet ground away from the midway. A guest can visit it without passing through a ride queue.
