The districts / The Rail Yard

Stand where the freight changes hands.

The public walk is outdoors. From the yard a visitor can follow the switching, see repair work, and watch freight move without boarding a ride.

At the turntable

The turntable gives the yard a center. Around it, the repair-yard walk makes tools, rolling stock, and the work of turning a locomotive legible from a public path outside the active workshops.

Passengers and freight

The covered platform puts passengers beside the warehouse and freight dock. From the public side you can see the freight office. Someone boarding with a wheelchair uses the same main platform as the rest of the party.

The timber train

The proposed timber-freight coaster takes its character from the yard. One person can head for the ride while another stays at the turntable. They can meet at the depot without either having to double back through a queue.
