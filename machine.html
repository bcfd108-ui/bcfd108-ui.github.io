The field guide / The Living Machine

Follow one thing through the park.

A load of timber gives the railroad a job. The mill changes its shape. A second freight movement takes the boards away. That chain of work is what Rimrock means by the Living Machine.

Follow the freight

A guest first sees the train from the arrival rise, sees its load in the mill pond, then finds lumber in the shipping yard. The branded log gives a child something specific to look for at each stop.

Follow the water

In Waterworks, the headgate release sets the current in motion. Across the wider design, channels, a reservoir, and the mill pond carry the water story outdoors. The different water systems have their own engineering and treatment requirements.

Follow a shift

A Shift Card picked up at the hub leads to places where the work can be seen: the mill office, the assay counter, the Waterworks plant window, and the Fire Hall. It also gives a family a reason to ask a worker what just happened.
