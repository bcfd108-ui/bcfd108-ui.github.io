The districts / Caribel Mill

Pick a log. Find its boards.

A branded log is the beginning of a guest’s visit to Caribel. Follow it through the pond and the steam mill, then look for its boards in the shipping yard.

At the pond

Freight cars deliver timber to the unloading deck. The upper Mill Pond holds the logs until they are sorted. A bull chain draws a log out of the water and into the mill.

Watch the cut

Guests look down from a gallery while the crew works the mill floor. A log rides the carriage to the saw. At the far end, the interest changes from the size of the tree to the stack of boards it yielded.

Lunch on the waterfront

The Wanigan takes its name and its long-table dining from the floating cookhouses that supplied log drives. Little Loggers and the planned forest-to-mill flume give families other ways into the same story.
