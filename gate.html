The districts / The Gate & Mountain House

From the farm lane to the carousel.

The first glimpse in the arrival design is a standpipe above the fields. The hotel and the rest of the park come into view farther up the rise.

The walk up

Water runs downhill beside the guest path. Up on the rim, a freight train can be seen hauling its load. The approach keeps the railway off the guest walk at grade.

Inside the doors

The hub combines a railroad station and a machine hall. The carousel is the first moving landmark inside. From here a family can collect a Shift Card, find Guest Services, rent a stroller, or head through the resort gate toward Firelands.

Mountain House

The restaurant belongs in the hub. Its timber porch gives people a place to sit together while the rest of the party comes through the gate. On the way out, the same porch becomes a last stop before the trip home.
