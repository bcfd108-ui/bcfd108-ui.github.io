The field guide / Dining at Rimrock

A table for each shift.

Coffee in a robe at the Waterworks gallery. A tray and a place on the bench at fire camp. Dinner by the hotel courtyard after dark. At Rimrock, where you sit says something about whose working day you’ve stepped into.

At the hotel

Skip’s is the proposed premium dining room. The diner serves the family meal, while quick service meets the early-coffee and between-swims crowd. The Waterworks Canteen puts a dry table within view of the water.

Along the park route

Mountain House is the porch inside the hub. Firelands has the Mess Hall for the midday rush. In Caribel, the Wanigan sets a communal table by the mill. At the Rail Yard, the General Store carries food suited to a depot bench.

Behind the dining rooms

A shared production kitchen is planned to supply outlets with distinct service styles. That choice connects dining-room design to deliveries, laundry, and the hotel’s year-round work.
