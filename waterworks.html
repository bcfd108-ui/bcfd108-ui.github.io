The districts / Company Waterworks

A day inside the 1935 waterworks.

The headgates open and the current begins to move. In Rimrock’s indoor water park, a family can float the Headrace, watch a raft come off the tower, and see the pumps that make the water move.

Follow the Headrace

The current channel passes the children’s Pumphouse and skirts the Reservoir, the family pool with a gradual entry. The Surge Tank holds water games. Rimrock Springs is the quieter end of the hall, with warm water and stone grottos.

Up the standpipe

One family climbs the standpipe together for a raft ride. Two kids carry mats up for a race. A body flume and a bowl complete the five rides, and the bowl finishes where someone at a gallery table can see the splash.

Look through the glass

The Works puts filtration and pumps behind viewing glass. The Canteen opens toward the gallery. Separate hotel and park-side entries support a hotel morning swim and planned day visits when the outdoor park is closed.
