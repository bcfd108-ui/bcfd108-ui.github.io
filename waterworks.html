The resort / Company Waterworks

Inside the proposed 1935-style waterworks.

At Rimrock Company Waterworks, opening the headgates would set the current in motion. Families could float the Headrace, watch a raft come off the tower and see the pumps at work.

Follow the Headrace

The Headrace passes the children’s Pumphouse and skirts The Reservoir, the indoor family pool with a gradual entry. The Surge Tank holds water games. Rimrock Springs is the quieter end of the hall, with warm water and stone grottos.

Up the standpipe

One family climbs the standpipe together for a raft ride. Two kids carry mats up for a race. A body flume and a bowl complete the five rides, and the bowl finishes where someone at a gallery table can see the splash.

Look through the glass

The Works puts filtration and pumps behind viewing glass. The Canteen opens toward the gallery. Separate hotel and park-side entries support a hotel morning swim and planned day visits when the outdoor park is closed.
